## Recent Insights

### Insight 1: Search Is Diversifying

Rand argues that users increasingly search on AI tools, social media, YouTube, and communities instead of only Google.

My takeaway:

Businesses should optimize content for multiple platforms and AI systems instead of relying solely on Google SEO.

---

### Insight 2: Audience Research Matters

Rand emphasizes understanding where audiences spend time and what influences their decisions.

My takeaway:

AI can generate content, but understanding the audience remains the foundation of successful marketing.

---

### Insight 3: Brand Building Is Essential

Rand believes strong brands perform better across search engines, AI assistants, and social media.

My takeaway:

AI-powered SEO should focus not only on rankings but also on building brand authority and trust.

## Why Rand Fishkin Is Important For AI-Powered SEO

Rand Fishkin provides a broader perspective on search and marketing. His insights help B2B SaaS companies prepare for a future where AI, social media, and traditional search coexist.
