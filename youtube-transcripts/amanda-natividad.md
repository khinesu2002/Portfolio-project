## Key Points

### Key Point 1: Zero-Click Content Is Growing

Amanda explains that users increasingly consume content directly on social media and AI platforms without clicking links.

**My takeaway:**

Businesses should create content that delivers value immediately instead of focusing only on website traffic.

---

### Key Point 2: AI Is Changing Search Behavior

People now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI assistants instead of searching Google for everything.

**My takeaway:**

SEO strategies should adapt to AI-powered search experiences and multiple discovery channels.

---

### Key Point 3: Audience Research Is Essential

Amanda emphasizes understanding where audiences spend time and what content formats they prefer.

**My takeaway:**

AI can create content quickly, but marketers still need strong audience research and distribution strategies.
